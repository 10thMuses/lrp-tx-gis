## Through-lines

1. **Power is the binding constraint, not chips or capital.** Microsoft says GPUs are
   sitting in inventory for lack of energization; turbine slots, not money, gate new gas.
2. **OBBBA/FEOC is bifurcating clean energy** — storage, 45Q CCS, and geothermal attract
   capital and premiums; resi-solar dealers, offshore wind, and green hydrogen retreat.
   A June 6 court vacatur restored the 5% safe harbor, making 2026 construction starts
   the decisive race against the July 4, 2026 cliff.
3. **Brownfield power trades at a deep discount to greenfield** — PJM gas changing hands
   at $743–1,327/kW vs >$2,000/kW new-build, while data-center demand bids up the curve.
4. **The Meta/Blue Owl $29.5B Hyperion SPV is the template** the rest of the hyperscaler
   field is now expected to copy.

## US Policy / Regulatory / Tax

### Tax — OBBBA follow-on

- **Court vacated IRS Notice 2025-42 on June 6, restoring the 5% safe harbor** for wind/
  solar >1.5 MW to establish beginning-of-construction (full vacatur; government likely to
  appeal/seek stay). Makes 2026 starts far easier against the **hard cliff: begin
  construction by July 4, 2026 OR placed-in-service by Dec 31, 2027** — and queue/permitting
  delays do not toll the placed-in-service backstop. — McGuireWoods, June 2026,
  mcguirewoods.com/client-resources/alerts/2026/6/federal-court-vacates-irs-notice-2025-42-restores-5-safe-harbor-for-wind-and-solar-projects/
- **FEOC material-assistance rule (Notice 2026-15) has split effective dates** — material
  assistance applies to construction starting on/after Jan 1, 2026, but the IP/"effective
  control" rule is **retroactive to July 4, 2025**, putting 2025 licensing spend at risk.
  MACR ramp: qualified facilities 40% (2026) → 45% → 60%; storage 55% → 60% → 75%. **Iron
  and steel excluded from the calc** — a carve-out favoring steel-heavy domestic projects.
  **Geothermal has no Identification Safe Harbor** (a loser). Treasury must publish
  PFE-specific safe-harbor tables by Dec 31, 2026. — Nixon Peabody, 2026-03-20,
  nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/03/20/foreign-entities-of-concern-the-material-assistance-rule-and-irs-notice-2026-15
- **O&G structural tailwind:** OBBBA made 100% bonus depreciation permanent for property
  placed in service after Jan 19, 2026, so tangible drilling costs expense in year one
  alongside the 100% IDC deduction; with the §469(c)(3) working-interest exemption, drilling
  losses offset wages and capital gains. (California has not conformed.) — BastaCPA, 2026,
  bastacpa.com/oil-gas-tax-breaks-explained-by-cpa/

### FERC / DOE — large load & co-location

- **FERC found PJM's tariff "unjust and unreasonable" and ordered three new co-located-load
  service tiers** (193 FERC ¶ 61,217; compliance filings due Feb 16). The non-obvious bite:
  a new **behind-the-meter "materiality threshold"** capping netting benefits — FERC found
  BTM netting "can result in improper cost shifting" — eroding the economics of parking a
  data center behind a generator to dodge transmission charges (3-yr transition,
  grandfathering). — Mintz, 2026-02-02,
  mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2151/2026-02-02-ferc-directs-pjm-issue-new-rules-co-location-power
- **FERC generic large-load rulemaking (RM26-4)** on a deadline to act by June 2026
  (loads >20 MW); SPP's "HILL" framework approved Jan 2026. **DOE's LPO now runs as the
  Office of Energy Dominance Financing** — 46 active loans + 25 conditional commitments,
  ~$118B. — FERC, ferc.gov/rm26-4; DOE, energy.gov/EDF

### Permitting & tariffs

- **CEQ eliminated its NEPA regulations; DOE moved NEPA procedures out of the CFR.** Most
  consequential for grid: **DOE rescinded NEPA/APA review for Presidential Permits** (the
  authorization for cross-border transmission lines). — Federal Register, 2026-02-24,
  federalregister.gov/documents/2026/02/24/2026-03708/national-environmental-policy-act-implementing-regulations
- **Section 232 metals tariffs restructured June 8:** 50% aluminum/steel/copper, 25%
  derivatives — but **grid equipment (transformers, switchgear) locked at 15% through Dec
  31, 2027.** Two mechanics worth knowing: goods made abroad entirely from US-origin metal
  pay 10%; a **15%-or-less metal-content de minimis** exempts a product entirely. — Green
  Worldwide, 2026, greenworldwide.com/section-232-tariff-reductions-for-agricultural-hvac-and-mobile-industrial-equipment-effective-june-8-2026/

## Space Economy → Domestic Land / Resource / Energy

### Texas land footprint

- **Starbase, TX annexing 7,133 acres** in Cameron County — growing the incorporated city
  ~9x (from ~927 to ~8,060 acres); advanced in a Feb 18 closed session. Separately, a
  federal land swap gives SpaceX **715 acres of the Lower Rio Grande Valley NWR** near Boca
  Chica for 683 acres of private land (approved early June) — among the largest refuge
  exchanges outside Alaska, now drawing a federal lawsuit from environmental/tribal groups.
  — RGV Business Journal, 2026-06; FWS, fws.gov/project/potential-land-exchange-spacex
- **Spaceport power as a bottleneck:** upgrading Cape Canaveral's electrical system is
  pegged at ~$275M, deemed insufficient for certain launch ops (one of six infrastructure
  constraints). No hard public data yet on a dedicated Starbase TX power source — a PUCT
  filing would confirm. — CBS Miami / Space Florida, 2026,
  cbsnews.com/miami/news/6-projects-eyed-to-increase-florida-space-launches/

### Datacenter-in-space — the land/grid displacement thesis

- **SpaceX filed with the FCC Jan 30 for an orbital data-center constellation of up to 1
  million satellites**, framing ~1M tonnes/yr of launches as ~100 GW of orbital AI compute
  — equivalent to ~20% of current US electricity that would otherwise sit on US land/grid
  (filed 3 days before the SpaceX/xAI merger closed Feb 2). — SpaceNews, 2026-01,
  spacenews.com/spacex-files-plans-for-million-satellite-orbital-data-center-constellation/
