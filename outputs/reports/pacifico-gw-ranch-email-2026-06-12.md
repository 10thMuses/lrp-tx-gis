**Subject:** Pacifico GW Ranch (Pecos County) — diligence: best permit position in Texas, weakest disclosed capital stack

Team —

Full report attached. We ran six research tracks (EN + JA, ~90 sources) on Pacifico Energy's GW Ranch, the 7.65 GW off-grid gas + 1.8 GW BESS + 750 MWac solar campus on 8,000+ acres on Hwy 18, ~17 miles north of Fort Stockton. Headlines:

**What's real.** TCEQ air permit issued Jan 26, 2026 — largest power-gen air permit in US history, ~5 months from public launch. Pecos County Ch. 312 abatement approved Jan 12. Fully islanded private grid: zero ERCOT queue, zero SB6 curtailment exposure, zero FERC. Dedicated 15-mile, 1 Bcf/d lateral to Waha — a demand sink sitting at the constrained hub while Waha printed 47 straight negative days this spring. As a permit + land + gas position, it's best-in-class among the Texas GW campuses.

**What's not there yet.** No anchor tenant (10 months post-launch), no named turbine OEM behind the "turbines secured" claim, no EPC, no announced project financing, no confirmed groundbreaking — "construction can start Q1 2026" came and went, and Pacifico was still presenting to Commissioners Court May 11. Buildout already slipped 2030 → 2031. Forbes puts the ask plainly: "all he needs is the $12 billion and the hyperscalers."

**Who's behind it (the part that wasn't clear).** The PR backer list — Goldman, Sumitomo, GE EFS, Shinsei, Dragon Capital — unpacks into placement agents, tax-equity buyers, and exited project-level co-investors. At parent level Pacifico is founder/family-controlled: Jamieson Group (CA family oil & gas/real estate) → Virginia Solar Group → "Pacifico Energy AM Holdings L.P." (66.7% of the Japan platform; unregistered in any US state, likely Cayman) plus a 33.3% management grant to the Japan CEO. No institutional sponsor anywhere in the chain. And the war chest is impaired: BofA ran a $1B+ sale of the Japanese solar platform starting Sept 2024 — KKR, Macquarie, and CIP all passed, the ask was cut ~70% to ~$200M, and no closing has been reported. The implicit "Japan proceeds fund Texas" story doesn't bridge to $12B.

**Land.** Tenure unconfirmed. Sixteen months of PR uses only possession-neutral language ("sited within 8,000+ acres") — never owns/acquired/optioned. No seller, ranch name, or price anywhere. The report includes a 30-minute close-out playbook: Pecos CAD owner search, TexasFile grantee search, and a TPIA request for the Jan 12 abatement agreement, which recites the property interest verbatim.

**Comp watch.** Same-county cautionary tale: CoreWeave terminated its 250 MW Project Horizon lease in March after Poolside's raise failed. Fermi is the category mark: -80%+ from IPO, class actions. Counter-signals nearby: Microsoft's reported 2,500 MW Reeves County site and LandBridge/PowerBridge's 2 GW at Waha. Also new: Pacifico's other Texas project (Fort Spunky, Hood County) had its concept plan revoked after a water denial — Pacifico is suing the county.

**Bottom line.** GW Ranch is an option on the Permian power thesis, not yet an executing project. De-risking signals to watch, in order: creditworthy anchor lease → financing close/Form D → named turbine OEM with delivery dates → EPC/groundbreaking → Japan sale outcome. Until one lands, treat the H1 2027 first-power date as a marketing artifact.
