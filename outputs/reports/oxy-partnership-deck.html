The situation, and OXY's pivot

  • Caramba North — ~1,300 ac, Pecos County — under evaluation to repurpose for a hyperscale data center.
  • Site control requires the shallow mineral estate (0–5,000 ft) and the working interest — to remove drilling / vibration risk on the surface.
  • On the Williams farm: minerals = OXY · working interest = Chevron.
  • OXY declined a cheap waiver — correctly: a data center runs ~$40B all-in per GW, so the mineral line item is immaterial to the hyperscaler's stack — and instead proposed a broader partnership across CCS, produced-water desalination, power, and its basin-wide minerals / working interests / fee lands.

Produced-water treatment & desalination

  • The Permian produced >20 MMbbl/d of water in 2024 (→ >26 by 2030). OXY is a top-tier handler.
  • Recycling at scale — South Curtis Ranch >50 MM bbl reused; Lost Tank (NM) up to 180k bbl/d.
  • TerraLithium direct-lithium-extraction (a brine-treatment train) + BHE JV — transferable to desalinating produced water into non-potable cooling makeup.
  • WES Reeves-County desal pilot — ~1,000 bbl/d reclaimed freshwater, a county over from Pecos.
Why it matters: treated produced water is the only scalable cooling source in Pecos that doesn't compete for Edwards-Trinity / Pecos Valley freshwater. A pilot here is a natural first joint project.

Deal comps & negotiation framing

  • OXY already contributes surface to a Permian DC-power project. Chevron's 2.5→5 GW, ~$7B AI-power campus (Pecos/Reeves; Microsoft offtaker) reportedly sits on land from TPL, Oxy USA & WaterBridge. (single source — verify in county records.)
  • Texas law — the mineral estate is dominant; the accommodation doctrine is weak (Lyle v. Midway Solar, a Pecos case) → a negotiated waiver / subordination from OXY + Chevron is required.
  • Benchmarks — TPL: $169M = 4,106 royalty ac (~$41k/ac) + 4,120 surface ac (~$41k/ac); producing Permian minerals $2–8k+/ac.
  • Framing — at ~$40B/GW, a shallow-mineral buyout + waiver is a rounding error. Leverage is alignment, not the doctrine.
