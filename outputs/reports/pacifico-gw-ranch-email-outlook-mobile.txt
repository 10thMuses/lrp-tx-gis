Mel —

Full diligence report attached on Pacifico Energy's GW Ranch — the 7.65 GW off-grid gas + 1.8 GW battery + 750 MWac solar campus on 8,000+ acres on Hwy 18, ~17 miles north of Fort Stockton. Key findings:

WHAT'S REAL. TCEQ air permit issued Jan 26, 2026 — the largest power-generation air permit in US history, about five months from public launch. Pecos County Chapter 312 abatement approved Jan 12. Fully islanded private grid: zero ERCOT queue, zero SB6 curtailment exposure, zero FERC. Dedicated 15-mile, 1 Bcf/d lateral to Waha — a demand sink sitting at the constrained hub while Waha printed 47 straight negative days this spring. As a permit + land + gas position, it's best-in-class among the Texas gigawatt campuses.

WHAT'S NOT THERE YET. No anchor tenant ten months after launch, no named turbine OEM behind the "turbines secured" claim, no EPC, no announced project financing, no confirmed groundbreaking — "construction can start Q1 2026" came and went, and Pacifico was still presenting to Commissioners Court on May 11. Full buildout has already slipped 2030 to 2031. Forbes put the ask plainly: "all he needs is the $12 billion and the hyperscalers."

WHO'S ACTUALLY BEHIND IT. The PR backer list — Goldman, Sumitomo, GE EFS, Shinsei, Dragon Capital — unpacks into placement agents, tax-equity buyers, and exited project-level co-investors. At parent level Pacifico is founder/family-controlled (Jamieson Group > Virginia Solar Group > "Pacifico Energy AM Holdings L.P.," unregistered in any US state) with a 33% management grant to the Japan CEO. No institutional sponsor anywhere in the chain. And the war chest is impaired: BofA ran a $1B+ sale of the Japanese solar platform starting Sept 2024 — KKR, Macquarie, and CIP all passed, the ask was cut ~70% to ~$200M, and no closing has been reported. The implicit "Japan proceeds fund Texas" story doesn't bridge to $12B.

LAND. Tenure unconfirmed. Sixteen months of PR uses only possession-neutral language ("sited within 8,000+ acres") — never owns/acquired/optioned. No seller, ranch name, or price anywhere. The report includes a 30-minute close-out: Pecos CAD owner search, TexasFile grantee search, and an open-records request for the Jan 12 abatement agreement, which recites the property interest verbatim.

COMP WATCH. Same-county cautionary tale: CoreWeave terminated its 250 MW Project Horizon lease in March after Poolside's raise failed. Fermi is the category mark: down 80%+ from IPO, securities class actions. Counter-signals nearby: Microsoft's reported 2,500 MW Reeves County site and LandBridge/PowerBridge's 2 GW at Waha. Also notable: Pacifico's other Texas project (Fort Spunky, Hood County) had its concept plan revoked after a water denial — Pacifico is suing the county.

BOTTOM LINE. GW Ranch is an option on the Permian power thesis, not yet an executing project. De-risking signals to watch, in order: creditworthy anchor lease, then financing close, then named turbine OEM with delivery dates, then EPC/groundbreaking, then the Japan sale outcome. Until one lands, treat the H1 2027 first-power date as a marketing artifact.
