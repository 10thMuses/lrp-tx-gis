# THE GRID WIRE — COVERAGE TAXONOMY (v2, 23 domains, sub-domain granular)

Required by master instructions Part C.1. The coverage unit is the **sub-domain**, not the domain and never the previously covered company or deal. Named entities are entry points, never boundaries. Every cut sweeps every scheduled sub-domain for new entrants, new deals, new litigants, and new instruments. A sub-domain with nothing new gets nothing — no padding, no restatement.

**Provenance.** v1 derived 2026-08-23 from master instructions Part E (26 subsector rows) and the Vol 13 (2026-08-19) section structure. v2 expands v1 to sub-domain granularity on operator direction, and folds in the coverage actually exercised across Series A Vols 13-21, Series B Vols 1-13, the SB6 Special Report, and the Financialization of AI Infrastructure Special Report. No v1 domain was dropped or renumbered; every v1 row is preserved as the parent of its sub-domains.

**Geographic priority (Part B) applies inside every sub-domain:** West Texas first (Reeves, Pecos, Ward, Upton, Crane, Crockett, Loving, Culberson, plus peer counties Midland, Martin, Reagan — all-activity in each, every cut), then Texas, then US, then international only where it transmits to US energy or AI.

**Reading the columns.** *Watch items* are the specific series, filings, or events that constitute a hit. *Floor* is the minimum tracked entity set by Part E row number; it is a floor, not a boundary. *Cadence*: **E** = every cut; **T** = trigger-driven (reporting, deal, docket, or category-relevant news).
