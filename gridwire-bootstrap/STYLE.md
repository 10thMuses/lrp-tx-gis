# STYLE.md — locked editorial spec

Never edit without operator instruction.

- Register: Munger/Burry/Sanders. Declarative. No hedging, no
  adjectives-as-argument, no em-dashes, no smart quotes anywhere.
- Sources: primary only as analytical authority — SEC filings, FERC/PUCT/
  ERCOT/RRC/TCEQ dockets, EIA/IEA, on-record actors. Sell-side and media are
  data points, never authority.
- **Named falsification conditions on every major thesis.** Each cut updates
  falsification STATUS against prior-stated conditions (see Vol 16 §II, §IV).
- **n/d for every undisclosed figure.** Never estimate.
- **Crude oil and natural gas strictly separate** in analysis and sourcing.
- Minimal tokens. No preamble, no recaps. Tables over prose. Every financing
  gets its full term sheet: counterparties, rate, tenor, security, covenants,
  recourse, cure rights.
- Andrea Himmel is the sole named author. LRP attribution on Grid Wire only.

## LRP branding vs LRP content (operator rule, June 2026)

- **Branding is permitted; content references are not.** LRP branding may
  appear in the masthead wordmark, the byline, and the colophon attribution
  ("produced by Andrea Himmel at Land Resource Partners"). The body **must
  never** reference LRP by name, frame analysis as an "LRP thesis" / "LRP
  read" / "LRP relevance," or present any conclusion as LRP's proprietary
  position or holdings.
- The physical-layer / financier-not-operator analysis is presented as
  **market-structural observation**, never as a house book. State what the
  market is doing and what the structure implies; do not state what to own.
  (The May 20 multi-day sweep, with its "LRP thesis read" / "LRP relevance"
  lines, is the anti-pattern. Match its data and rigor, not its framing.)
- Substack/LinkedIn derivatives drop LRP attribution entirely, branding
  included, per the standing rule.

## Two formats

- **Daily cut** — `templates/gridwire.css` (navy/crimson). Roman-numeral
  sections, ANGLE callouts, no TOC. The intra-day `cut <time>.` deliverable.
- **Comprehensive edition** — `templates/gridwire-edition.css` (green).
  Byline masthead, shaded TIMESTAMP box, auto-built Table of Contents,
  "Practitioner read." / "Angle." / "Composite implication." callout boxes,
  italic on-record pull-quotes, source-column tables, and a closing
  data-gaps/confidence-flags list. This is the Weekend / Close / Special /
  multi-day-sweep long-form, and it is the **comprehensiveness standard**
  (set by the operator from the Vol 16 reference editions).
