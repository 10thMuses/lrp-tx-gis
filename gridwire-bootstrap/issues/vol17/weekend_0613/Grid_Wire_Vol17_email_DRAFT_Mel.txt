II. THE BOND MOVE AND THE AI FINANCING STACK
~5% 30Y / ~4.4% 10Y is the benchmark every stack layer prices off; the ceasefire eased it without changing the regime. IG DDTLs reprice first; SPV bonds compound base + spread; neocloud HY refi math tightens against 4-5yr GPU collateral on 7yr contracts; powered-shell project finance is hit most. The ceasefire is the first potential circuit-breaker in the reflexive loop (energy shock -> inflation -> long end -> AI financing cost). Both rate branches are negative for the financed layer: higher-for-longer raises cost directly; financial repression debases the real return on long-dated levered cash flows. Physical inputs carry a higher cost of capital better than a levered claim does.
